# The shape of Still

Open Still and write. Arrival resumes the current page; the wordmark or native Cmd+N starts an empty one. Neither a title nor a category is required. Before can be encountered without learning how the collection is arranged.

The Lists provide the form: colon titles, authored breaks, blank space, indentation, voices, interruptions, and long endings. The room leaves those shapes intact. It does not turn voices into conversations with a machine or fragments into a common template. Imported writing is editable; its earlier words and source relationships remain available.

## Place and light

Provenance gives Before a geography. Pieces from one source artifact occupy the closest neighborhood. Other pieces from the same physical notebook are near; distinct notebooks sit elsewhere. The two source folders inside the current Notebook 1 belong to one place. These positions describe source relationships, not a timeline.

The place name is a small whisper at the edge of the room. Opening it reveals scattered notebook names in darkness, with Now below them. There are no points, edges, folder controls, counts, or breadcrumbs. Only notebooks that hold writing appear. The landscape covers the page without destroying the editor or its pending words.

Light belongs to the room rather than a settings screen. One continuous control moves between smooth dark greys and blues, with no grain or texture. It persists between visits. Place changes the light's position and adds a small tonal difference; it does not assign a mood to the words or replace the writer's chosen light. Letters, beside Light, adjusts the writing through a useful 17–33px range, with the existing 21px midpoint. Titles and nearby language change at their own restrained scales; navigation and notebook names remain quiet. In native fullscreen the room keeps its quiet colors without fading readable text below useful contrast. The writing surface uses a very faint shadow edge when focused; other controls keep their visible keyboard focus, and increased-contrast or forced-color preferences retain a clear editor outline; Escape lets Tab leave the indented writing surface. The map is a modal keyboard region, and narrow windows present its existing places as a scrollable sequence.

## Attraction

Writing in Now can draw a small field of Before toward the edges after a pause. Local computation looks for literal language among the historical pieces, giving uncommon words and phrases more weight. Generic isolated matches stay quiet. The newest words matter more than those farther back, and an existing presence has enough stability to avoid switching at every small change. A second presence requires independent language and a different source artifact. At most two become perceptible, and narrow or short rooms show only one.

Each presence has a stable place and distance. Stronger language can bring it closer; changing direction lets it recede. Replacements wait for departing words to leave. Pausing leaves the field settled rather than making it perform. Following a fragment brings the List forward from that side; near and far crossings move with different restraint. Escape returns to the same caret and scroll position. The field asks for no response, keeps no engagement score, and generates no interpretation. Editing or restoring an old List changes what can be drawn toward Now.

The field is scarce; weakly related pieces remain imperceptible. Its positions describe a relationship to the current words rather than a ranking. Source neighborhoods remain an open geography. Dark light has no theme names or swatches, and motion settles after an arrival or crossing. Cards, dashboards, and explanations of why a piece was recommended would make the interface compete with the writing.

## A word travels

Threads is a deliberate place to follow literal language between historical Lists
and saved pages. A few recurring words offer entrances. Two original passages can
meet across the room, then unfold into complete pages; other passages remain
reachable below. These are authored stanzas, never generated collages. A source and
its saved edition remain one family for discovery and pairing. Nothing is filed,
scored, rearranged, or saved by wandering here.

The page follows the room's light and letters. Its columns become a reading sequence
in a narrow window. Saved reading can also offer one historical companion beneath
the words, using the same scarce literal evidence as the existing field. Its own
historical original is excluded. Now continues to hold the writer's current page.

## A local body

An AppKit window and WKWebView give the existing Phoenix application a native macOS body: Dock presence, a generous first window, remembered size and position, ordinary title-bar zoom, and native fullscreen. The title bar belongs to AppKit, with a Window menu for the same native actions. Browser swipe navigation, pinch zoom, and link previews are disabled in the shell. Native arrow and text cursors remain ordinary. The shell starts and stops a bundled local BEAM backend. Docker and ordinary host development remain available against the same SQLite collection.

Autosave and local recovery storage protect the transition from typed words to saved words. A visible Save action confirms an immediate save without leaving the editor. Saved writing reopens new pages and changed imported Lists in most-recently-saved order; All writing still includes the whole collection. The native File menu offers Save (Cmd+S) and Open Writing (Cmd+O). Optimistic locking rejects stale edits; Earlier words preserve changed writing. The browser and native WebView keep separate unsent copies, while their saved writing shares the database. SQLite backups and JSON export provide explicit ways to leave with the words. The native File menu remembers a chosen writing folder with a filesystem bookmark; its recovery identity survives a folder rename. Missing folders never silently become new collections.

There are no accounts, outside AI calls, telemetry, remote fonts, or uploaded writing. Persistence, recovery, search, geography, and textual relationships live in contexts. LiveView handles interaction; browser JavaScript remains limited to editor recovery and keyboard behavior. Reduced-motion preferences keep the room still.
